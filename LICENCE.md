## 🧩 About

**Delta Force Map Overlay** is a comprehensive map overlay for Delta Force featuring real-time radar, enemy position tracking, minimap enhancements, tactical markers, and objective indicators.

## ✨ Features

### 🗺️ Map & Radar
- Real-time Radar — display enemy positions on a radar
- Minimap Enhancer — enlarge and customize the minimap
- Enemy Tracker — show enemy movements with directional indicators
- Objective Markers — highlight objectives and extraction points

### 🎯 Tactical Aids
- Tactical Markers — place custom markers for team coordination
- Distance Indicator — show distance to marked targets
- Safe Zone Overlay — visualize safe zones and danger areas
- Loot Highlight — highlight lootable containers and items

### ⚙️ Customization
- Custom Colors — change radar colors and icon styles
- Hotkey Configuration — rebind all overlay hotkeys

- Multi-Monitor Support — extend overlay across screens
